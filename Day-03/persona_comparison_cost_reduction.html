Side-by-side comparison: CFO vs HR Director persona responses to the same cost-reduction question

Same prompt →
"We need to reduce team costs by 20% next quarter. What should we do?"
CF
Act as a CFO
Numbers-first lens
RESPONSE FOCUS
Headcount analysis
Audit fully-loaded cost per role. Identify contractor vs FTE ratio for immediate flex.
Vendor & tooling cuts
SaaS stack audit — cancel unused seats, renegotiate annual contracts, consolidate overlapping tools.
Freeze non-essential spend
Travel, events, discretionary hiring — pause and re-evaluate against quarterly targets.
Target: -20% by week 12
Model 3 scenarios (optimistic / base / downside) and present board with break-even analysis.
Tone
Precise. Uses percentages, timelines, ROI framing. No emotion.
HR
Act as an HR Director
People-first lens
RESPONSE FOCUS
Voluntary options first
Offer unpaid leave, reduced hours, or sabbaticals before any involuntary decisions.
Transparent communication
Hold all-hands before decisions leak. Uncertainty damages trust more than bad news does.
Protect key talent
Identify flight-risk top performers. Cuts that save now and cost twice in rehiring are a false saving.
Legal & compliance check
Review notice periods, severance obligations, and regional labor laws before any restructuring.
Tone
Empathetic. Uses words like "trust", "morale", "retention". Balances legal risk.
BIGGEST OBSERVATION
Same question. Same Claude. Two completely different worldviews. The CFO never mentioned people's feelings. The HR Director never mentioned a spreadsheet. The role didn't change the facts — it changed which facts Claude thought mattered.
