Section 1: What is Prompt Engineering?
Prompt Engineering is the skill of writing clear, specific instructions to get the best possible output from an AI tool like Claude.
Think of it like giving directions. If you tell someone "go somewhere nice," they might end up anywhere. But if you say "take the second left, drive 2 km, and stop at the café on the corner," they arrive exactly where you want. Prompts work the same way — the more precise your instructions, the better your result.
Why it matters with AI tools like Claude:
AI responds to what you give it. A vague input produces a vague output. A well-structured prompt gives Claude the context, format, tone, and goal it needs to genuinely help you. You don't need to be a programmer — you just need to communicate more intentionally.

Section 2: Weak vs Improved Prompt
Weak Prompt:

"Write something about marketing."

This gives Claude almost no direction. The result could be a definition, a story, a tweet, a 5,000-word essay — anything. You'll likely get something generic and unhelpful.
Improved (Engineered) Prompt:

"Write a 3-paragraph LinkedIn post for a small business owner explaining why consistent social media marketing matters. Use a professional but friendly tone, and end with a call to action encouraging people to follow for weekly tips."

This tells Claude: the format (LinkedIn post), the audience (small business owners), the topic (consistency in social media), the tone (professional but friendly), the length (3 paragraphs), and the goal (CTA). The output will be dramatically more useful.

Section 3: Three Practical Benefits of Better Prompts

Save time — You get the right answer faster, with fewer back-and-forth corrections.
Higher quality output — Specific prompts produce content that matches your actual needs in tone, length, and format.
More creative results — When you give Claude context and constraints, it can be smarter and more targeted in its suggestions — not just more words, but the right words.
