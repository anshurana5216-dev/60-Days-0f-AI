 AI Shark Tank Simulator

An AI-powered startup pitching platform where entrepreneurs can present their ideas and receive realistic investor-style feedback. The simulator acts like a panel of Sharks, evaluating business concepts, market potential, revenue models, scalability, and investment readiness.

 Features
AI-generated Shark Tank experience
Startup pitch evaluation
Investor-style follow-up questions
Business model and market analysis
Funding and growth strategy feedback
Interactive and user-friendly interface
 Tech Stack
React.js
JavaScript
AI Integration (Claude/OpenAI API)
CSS/Tailwind CSS

 Purpose

Help founders, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs validate ideas, improve pitches, and prepare for real investor conversations.

 Future Enhancements
Multiple AI investor personalities
Pitch scoring system
Startup report generation
Pitch history and analytics
