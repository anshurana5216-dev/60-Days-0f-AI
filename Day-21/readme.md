# Digital Footprint Intelligence Dashboard

A modern cybersecurity-inspired dashboard that visualizes a user's digital footprint, privacy exposure, and estimated data collection risks across major online platforms.

## Features

* Digital Footprint Score
* Privacy Score
* Exposure Heatmap
* Company Exposure Ranking
* Data Collection Matrix
* Risk Radar
* Digital Twin Profile
* Most Valuable Data Assets
* Privacy Improvement Simulator
* Privacy Recommendations

## Disclaimer

This project does not access private databases or personal account data. All insights are generated from user-provided services and estimation-based privacy modeling.

## Inspiration
